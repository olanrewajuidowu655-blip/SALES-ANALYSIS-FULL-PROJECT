# sales_analysis_full_project

## Project Overview
    This project analyzes short-term sales analysis activity over a 6-month period to derive actionable insights from sales data, that support data-driven decision-making in pricing, inventory management, and market expansion.

## Business Objectives
    - Identify products that contribute to the highests share of total revenue 
    - Evaluate sales performance across different locations to identify high and under-performing regions
    - Examine sales trend over time in order to figure out overall revenue patterns and anomalities
    - Analyze how pricing affects sales performance using pricing, quantity and total revenue relationship
    
## Dataset
    The dataset contains sales records including:
    - Date
    - Location
    - Product category
    - Product
    - Quantity sold
    - Unit price
    - Total sales
 
## Tools used
    - Excel (for preparing dataset)
    - SQL (for data cleaning and analysis)
    - Power BI (for visualization)
   
## Business questions
    - Which products contribute to 80% of total revenue
    - Which locations generate the highest revenue and which are under-performing
    - How does sales change over time
    - Product performance versus pricing
   
     
 ## Insights
    - 9 out of 13 products: Ice Cream, Cake slice, Sausage Roll, Chicken Pie, Refuel Regular, Spicy Rice and Chicken, Juice, Meat Pie and Jollof Rice Combo, drive 80% of total revenue. Stock consistency and availability of these items should be a top operational priority as any disruption directly impacts the majority of revenue
    - Victoria Island, Ikeja, Surulere, and Lekki are the top performing locations while Ajah and Yaba are underperforming. A focused operational and commercial review of what the top branches are executing well (foot traffic, staffing, or local demand) should be replicated in Ajah and Yaba to close the performance gap
    - Chicken Pie, Ice Cream, Sausage Roll, and Refuel Regular justify their premium pricing with strong revenue performance. Juice, Meat Pie, and Coke are priced above average but underdelivering on revenue, signaling a pricing misalignment. Cake Slice stands out as a low price, high performance product with untapped pricing potential. Jollof Rice combo, Spicy Rice and Chicken, Refuel max, Fanta and Water are low price goods with low performance, stocking should be monitored to avoid revenue tie up
    - Sales peaked in March at 505 units, representing the sharpest month on month growth of 65 units. Growth continued mildly into April before a steady decline through May and a significant drop of 217 units in the month of June. Category leadership aslo shifted across the period; Meals dominated throughout January, February, and March while desserts dominated in April and May then in the month of June, snacks emerged at the top, reflecting a clear shift in customer demand over time.
